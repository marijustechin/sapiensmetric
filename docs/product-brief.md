# Product brief — Sapiens Metric

## Purpose

Sapiens Metric is a serious cognitive-ability and knowledge-assessment
platform. Today it reports **performance on specified, versioned tasks** in a
defensible, explainable way — not to entertain. The long-term goal is that,
with reliability and validity evidence, task performance may eventually be
interpreted as cognitive-ability or knowledge constructs for a specific
intended use. Until that evidence exists, no score is labelled an ability or
knowledge estimate (see `docs/claims-ladder.md`).

## Target audiences

- Individuals who want a structured, explainable report of their performance
  on specified tasks (not an IQ number).
- Educators and researchers interested in the underlying items, scoring rules,
  and methodology.
- Organisations (future, post-validation) that may use assessments for
  screening or development — only after norming and validity evidence exist.

## Languages

- Product UI and documentation: Lithuanian and English.
- Test content is handled separately per language-neutral, Lithuanian, and
  English item sets (see `docs/assessment-principles.md`).

## Non-goals

- It is **not** a quiz/entertainment website.
- It does **not** produce a real IQ score.
- It does **not** provide clinical diagnosis or medical/psychological advice.
- It does **not** provide hiring recommendations.
- It does **not** claim scientific validation before validation and norming
  exist.
- It does **not** copy or reconstruct proprietary/protected instruments
  (Raven, Mensa, WAIS, and similar).

## Guiding principles

1. Seriousness over engagement.
2. Explainability: scoring must be transparent and reproducible.
3. Validation-gated claims: claims follow evidence, never precede it.
4. Language fairness: content is treated as language-neutral or localised, and
   that distinction is explicit.
